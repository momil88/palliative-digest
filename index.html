"Full paper" links go through USF Libraries' OpenAthens sign-in, so paywalled articles open with your USF credentials. If your library gives you a different proxy prefix (for example, the USF Health proxy), paste it here. Use {url} where the article address goes.

Paper data comes from PubMed (National Library of Medicine), Fast Facts from the Palliative Care Network of Wisconsin (mypcnow.org), and the podcast from GeriPal. Summaries are written from abstracts and show notes, so check the original before changing practice.
